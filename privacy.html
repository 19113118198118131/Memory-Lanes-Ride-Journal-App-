Privacy Policy

Effective 11 August 2026

Memory Lanes is a ride journal. We collect the information needed to record, save and synchronise the rides and social features you choose to use.

Information we handle

This can include account details, recorded location and GPX data, ride notes and moments, route plans, optional group ride details and optional live-location updates. Live location is off by default and can be stopped from the ride flow.

How information is used

We use your information to provide ride recording, your private library, optional cloud sync, navigation, analytics, group coordination and support. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Sharing and visibility

Your ride library is private unless you choose to share a ride or join a group activity. Group ride hosts and members only see the details needed for that ride. Ride Mesh and live sharing are optional nearby coordination features, not emergency services.

Storage and security

Memory Lanes stores account and ride data with Supabase. Data is protected with access controls designed to limit records to the signed-in rider. No online service can promise absolute security, so keep your account credentials private.

Your choices

You can export your account data from Account. You can request account deletion from Account, which signs you out and starts a deletion request. We may retain limited records where required for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion follow-up, use the Memory Lanes support page.
